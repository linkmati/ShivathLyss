# Shelf Contract: 7 - DM / Lore Building (Behind-the-Screen World Secrets)

Specialized contract for DM-exclusive lore, unrevealed world truths, villain plots, and campaign countdown clocks.

## Key Purpose
- Maintain strict separation between public lore (`1 - Mundo`) and DM-only truths (`7 - DM/Lore Building`).
- Develop evolving factions, antagonists, secret agendas, and sandbox regions (e.g. `Vespria.md`).

## Workflow for DM Lore Development
1. **The Secret / Truth**: Core revelation unknown to players.
2. **Faction / Antagonist Moves**: What villains do if the players take no action (Fronts / Clocks).
3. **Clues & Trail**: Breadcrumbs placed into future sessions to let players discover the truth.
4. **Integration**: Link to relevant `1 - Mundo/` entities without modifying public player-facing notes.

## Context Boundaries
- **Load When**: Brainstorming campaign secrets, plotting villain arcs, developing unrevealed regions.
- **Do NOT Load**: Player-facing journals or public wikis unless reading as baseline.
- **Token Target**: 2k–4k tokens.
