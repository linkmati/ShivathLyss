# Shelf Contract: 2 - Historia (Chronicles & Timelines)

Record Library tracking temporal events across the campaign world.

## Structure
- `Pasado/`: Historical ages, ancient wars, founding events, genesis myths.
- `Presente/`: Ongoing era, current geopolitical tensions, active world events.
- `Futuro/`: Prophecies, potential timelines, impending threats.

## Context Boundary Rules
- **Load When**: Checking historical dates, sequence of past events, or prophecies.
- **Do NOT Load**: Full world lore (`1 - Mundo`) or player sheets unless directly referenced by an event.
- **Token Target**: 2k–4k tokens per era inquiry.
