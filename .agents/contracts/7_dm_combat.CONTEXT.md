## Boss Design Pipeline
1. **Concept & Theme**: Boss identity, environment, phase count (1-3 phases).
2. **Action Economy & Resource**: Turn actions, reactive actions, temporal milestone actions, accumulation resources (e.g. charges/stacks).
3. **Core Traits Selection**: Lethality, Adaptability, Combo Synergy, Mitigation/Defense, Control/Denial, Guaranteed Effects.
4. **Phase Transitions**:
   - *Phase 1 (Interactive)*: Sabotageable components, counterable mechanics.
   - *Phase 2/3 (Consolidated)*: Inherent powers, high lethality, direct reactivity.
5. **Output**: Formatted using `.agents/templates/boss_statblock.md`.

## Context Boundaries
- **Load When**: Designing bosses, tuning monster statblocks, building tactical combat encounters.
- **Do NOT Load**: Unrelated world lore or player mission logs.
- **Token Target**: 2k–5k tokens.
