# Shelf Contract: 1 - Mundo (Worldbuilding & Cosmology)

Knowledge Bundle for world geography, deities, factions, cosmology, and lore.

## Structure
- `Shivath.md`: Core world overview and geography.
- `Panteones/`: Deities, gods, cosmic forces.
- `Patrones/`: Patrons, entities, pacts.
- `Mazdas/` & `Angramanio/`: Planetary forces / cosmologies.
- `Lunas/`: Moons and celestial bodies.
- `Organizaciones/`: Factions, guilds, sects, orders.
- `Nivel Tecnologico.md`: Technology level and development constraints.
- `Biblioteca de Amunuon.md`: Mythological library lore.

## Context Boundary Rules
- **Load When**: Researching setting background, deity lore, faction allegiances, or geography.
- **Do NOT Load**: Player character sheets (`3 - Personajes/Jugadores`), DM encounter stats (`7 - DM/Combat`), or unrelated history branches.
- **Token Target**: 2k–4k tokens per targeted entity.
