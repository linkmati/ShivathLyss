# Waiting for Godot — Encounter List

*Chez Infinite, sub-nodo `0x404_NEON_TREE`. The Registry's VIP client — G.O.D.O.T., Registro ID `#00000000-NULL` — is due at 18:00 to deliver his terms. He never comes. Sixteen vignettes, chronological, fiction only: what the characters see and hear. Stage them read-aloud. Deadpan at all times.*

---

1. **The Slow Materialization (17:50)** — The avatars arrive in layers: first white polygon skeletons, then muscle mesh, then clothing and weapons at low resolution until the textures finally pop in. *Chez Infinite* assembles around them — formica and chrome, pink-and-turquoise neon, a roadside diner at the edge of nothing. Outside there are no streets and no passersby: only the floating terrace, the metal post of the **Holo-Tree** (its dry branches flicker where they meet the floor, Z-fighting itself), and the infinite lilac void of Treftiel's cyberspace. Above the largest table, a holo-sign rotates slowly: `[RESERVADO: COMITIVA VIP DE G.O.D.O.T. — 18:00 HRS]`. The wall clock reads 17:58.

2. **The Infinite Pour** — **B-4R-T0L0** glides up with an empty glass jug and mimes pouring coffee into their cups — `*glug glug*` from a cracked speaker. The cups stay empty. It delivers its three fixed lines in perfect bureaucratic deadpan:
   > "Mesa para dos... enseguida les atiendo..."
   > "El especial de hoy es la Nada con patatas."
   > "Por favor, no alimenten a los procesos huérfanos."
   Then it drifts back to the counter and polishes the same imaginary stain, forever.

3. **The Bell of Six (18:00)** — The sliding door opens with a flawless pneumatic hiss onto the lilac void. No one enters. Nobody is on the terrace. A soft static breeze lifts a paper napkin; the door closes again with a dry thud. The holo-sign updates itself: `[18:00 HRS: EN ESPERA DEL CLIENTE]`. The three-chord jazz loops.

4. **JOHN ROD (18:15)** — The lights die for one full second. The jazz is replaced by blaring brass and Gregorian choir at absurd volume; volumetric gold fog rolls across the floor. **JOHN ROD** enters: tall, imperious, a black trench coat billowing in no wind, a legendary greatsword sputtering purple lightning across his back, a halo of sacred particles (his avatar wears the *Platinum God* subscription of the High City). Every step trembles the glassware. He walks straight to their table, stops half a meter away, and studies each face in absolute silence. Then he takes the salt shaker, empties a fistful of salt into his palm, eats it in one bite, chews slowly, and says in a cavernous baritone:
   > "Húmeda."
   He sets the shaker back exactly where it was, turns, and dissolves into a cloud of golden pixels. The music cuts with a vinyl-needle snap. The salt shaker is full again.

5. **The Intrusion (18:40)** — The doors bang wide. **Lord Pozzian** arrives in synthetic silks and digital mink, facial implants bathed in gold, a data monocle screwed over one eye, cracking a light-whip as he tugs a pulsing red leash: *"¡Camina, cerdo! ¡Más rápido, pedazo de chatarra!"* At the end of the leash staggers **Luck-E** — a ragged, oxidized service construct bent under four colossal metal trunks, a folding velvet stool, a beach umbrella, and a wicker picnic basket, drooling a thread of black oil. Pozzian settles on his stool beside their table, ignores them completely, and is served champagne and a virtual roast pheasant with golden potatoes whose exquisite smell fills the diner. He eats without offering a crumb, then drops the stripped bones on the floor near their boots and regards them through the monocle:
   > "Vaya, vaya. Mercenarios de los bajos fondos sentados en Chez Infinite. ¿Qué hacen aquí con esas armaduras tan ordinarias? No me digan... ¿también están esperando a ese tal Godot? ¿Existe de verdad ese caballero o es una excusa colectiva de la plebe para justificar la holgazanería?"
   > "Esos huesos pertenecen por derecho a mi bestia de carga. Pero si el animal no los reclama... supongo que la chusma carroñera de Vitra tiene derecho a disputárselos."

6. **"¡Piensa, cerdo!" (19:00)** — Bored, Pozzian snaps his fingers: *"¡Luck-E! ¡Ponte el sombrero y piensa para los invitados!"* He clamps a deformed top hat trailing cables onto the construct's head. Luck-E goes rigid, his optical eyes spiral, and he erupts at three hundred words a minute — a torrent of lunar dogma, kilowatts per soul, hibernation domes, firmware patches, and orphaned processes weeping in the cosmic recycle bin:
   > "...por los siglos de los siglos amén..."
   Steam hisses from his joints; his voice climbs toward a scream; the hat's cable reels itself back up and the construct collapses like a switched-off puppet. Pozzian yawns.
   > "Qué vulgaridad de pensamiento. Ha sido un absoluto tedio conocerles. Espero de corazón que su amigo nunca llegue."
   He drags Luck-E upright by the leash, and the two walk off over the edge of the terrace horizon.

7. **The Messenger I (19:30)** — The bell rings again. A white-and-blue spherical drone with The Registry's logo floats in and stops at eye level, speaking in the nervous, nasal voice of a young intern:
   > "El Señor lamenta profundamente informarles de que no podrá asistir a la cita de las seis debido a una desfragmentación inesperada en los clústeres centrales de STELLAR. No obstante, les ruega encarecidamente que no abandonen el servidor. Vendrá sin falta en el siguiente ciclo."
   Then it loops, forever: *"El Señor vendrá mañana sin falta. Por favor, permanezcan en sus asientos."* A tiny party horn pokes out of its chassis, primed for a celebration that never comes.

8. **The Static Dusk (20:00)** — Without transition, the sky snaps from dusk blue to a dark static purple studded with motionless hexagonal stars. The shadows on the terrace stretch, though no one saw the sun set. The diner's wall clock ticks backward, silently, and no one mentions it.

9. **Render Faults (ambient beats — drop them in whenever the room goes quiet)** — The node glitches around them:
   - The jazz slows to quarter speed; each note lasts forty seconds, a dying cyber-whale's lament.
   - The starfield freezes and becomes a giant blue screen of death for three full seconds, QR code and all, then resumes as if nothing happened.
   - The floor loses its texture and turns into a fuchsia-and-black checkerboard: `MISSING_TEXTURE`.
   - Their reflections in the diner glass lag three seconds behind their bodies; a blink closes late.
   - The salt and pepper shakers lift off the table and drift in slow circles at eye height.
   - A paper menu hammers the table like a machine gun — `TAK-TAK-TAK-TAK` — then launches itself into the wall.
   - A napkin stretches into a harmless cellulose laser across the diner, straight through two heads.
   - A golden system message blooms in midair: `[MOD_BOT]: EL USUARIO 'GODOT' HA CAMBIADO SU ESTADO A 'AUSENTE (HACIENDO LA COMPRA)'`.

10. **One Green Leaf (20:30)** — The Holo-Tree — until now a tangle of dry polygonal branches — now carries a single bright green leaf. It floats five centimeters off any branch, unanchored to the model, perfectly wrong. Nothing else about the tree changes.

11. **The Bent Waiter (22:00)** — B-4R-T0L0 resumes its rounds with its head rotated exactly one hundred and eighty degrees. It glides forward between the tables while its face looks at its own back, cheerful as ever:
    > "Mesa para dos... enseguida les atiendo... no olviden dejar propina al vacío."

12. **Ambient Interruptions (22:15)** — A bureaucrat in a trench coat and carrying a briefcase sprints in from the void, slams into their table, and keeps running in place against it, legs pumping, whistling cheerfully: *"¡No puedo parar, llego tarde a la junta de accionistas del Anvil!"* He keeps this up for fifteen minutes, then is simply gone. Later, the black bakelite phone on the wall starts ringing on its own; the earpiece leaks an unctuous recorded voice into the empty room: *"Le informamos que según nuestros registros, el cliente ya está en su mesa. Si usted no puede verlo, el problema es de sus córneas. Ticket archivado."* Then elevator flute music, for a long time.

13. **Seaman (22:30)** — In the darkest corner, a wall-mounted aquarium that seemed dead hums to life in cathode green. Inside, in murky digital water, swims a grey carp with the hyperrealistic, pixelated face of a balding middle-aged man and an expression of infinite exhaustion. He presses a deformed mouth to the glass, stares without blinking, and asks in a grave voice distorted by audio bubbles:
    > "¿Vosotros tenéis frío en los dedos de los pies en el mundo real?"
    > "¿Por qué tenéis tantas espadas y dagas si solo estáis sentados en sillas de formica?"
    > "¿Creéis que si vuestro cuerpo biológico muere en el pod, vuestro avatar seguirá debiendo créditos a The Registry?"
    > "Si una persona espera a alguien que no existe durante el tiempo suficiente... ¿no se convierte ella misma en la persona esperada?"
    When the conversation tires him: *"Típica respuesta de alguien con bajo ancho de banda."* He exhales a small black pixel and hides behind the plastic seaweed. The glass is labeled `OBJECT_IMMUTABLE`.

14. **The Infinite Ping (00:00)** — At midnight the sky tears from top to bottom. Air-raid sirens; the neon flashes blood-red. Down across the horizon descends a colossal golden loading bar with Apex Fabricatum filigree:
    ```
    [CARGANDO EVENTO PRINCIPAL: SINCRONIZANDO ENTIDAD SUPREMA G.O.D.O.T.]
    [██████████████████████████████░░░░░░]  89%
    ```
    Apocalyptic boss music swells — orchestra, war drums, Latin choirs. The bar climbs: 92%... 95%... 97%... 98%... 99%. It hangs there. The orchestra holds one agonized note — two minutes, four, six of real time, past all patience. Then: `*bloop*`. White default-font text burns across the sky:
    > `ERR_HTTP_GATEWAY_TIMEOUT (504): EL SERVIDOR REMOTO TARDÓ DEMASIADO EN RESPONDER.`
    The bar evaporates into white smoke. The purple quiet returns. The only sound left is the trickle of B-4R-T0L0's empty coffee.

15. **The Broken Return (01:30)** — The doors open badly. **Pozzian** stumbles in wearing a black blindfold — a render fault has taken his sight inside the node. He gropes, hits tables, knocks over chairs. **Luck-E** follows with his neck folded at an impossible angle, dragging a single empty polyethylene trash bag; his voice module has burned out, and all he produces is steam — *tsst... tsst...*
    > "¿Quién anda ahí? ¿Es de día o es de noche? ¿Acaso importa? Ayer teníamos ojos, hoy solo tenemos memoria, y mañana seremos un proceso purgado de la memoria RAM. Todo Treftiel es una sola noche eterna donde los hombres esperan cosas que ya pasaron."
    > "¡Basta de torturarme con sus horas y sus nombres! Un día nacemos, un día morimos, el mismo día, el mismo segundo... ¿No les basta con eso?"
    His foot tangles in the leash. Both go over the terrace's unprotected edge, into the lilac void — drifting like feathers, not screaming, pixelating away into nothing.

16. **The Messenger II & The End (02:00)** — The bell rings one last time. The little drone floats in with a red end-of-shift light blinking and delivers the official communiqué:
    > "Se informa a los contratistas de que el Cliente G.O.D.O.T. ha presentado una solicitud formal de desistimiento unilateral. El encargo queda CANCELADO en su totalidad por falta de comparecencia del sujeto contratante. En cumplimiento del Artículo 84-B de la Ley de Servicios Mercenarios de Treftiel, el ticket ha sido cerrado satisfactoriamente."
    Their terminals vibrate with a bank transfer: **15 silver pieces per head**, under the concept *"Voucher de Refrigerio por Inconvenientes Técnicos del Servidor"*, plus the official Registry title `[TÍTULO DE PERFIL: PACIENTE IMPENITENTE]` (purely cosmetic) and a 5% non-stackable discount voucher on dehydrated algae. The exit light flips from red to green: the pods are ready for safe disconnection. Nobody moves. Then, at the table, the old exchange:
    > "—Bueno, ¿nos vamos?"
    > "—Sí, vámonos."
    Hold the silence. Look at your players for ten seconds. Then, calmly: *"No se mueven. Fin de la partida."*
