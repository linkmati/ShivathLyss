# Combat Traits (Sistema Marcial de Arcadum)

## Death
- **Ruthless**: This creature may choose to execute a downed player if it is convenient.
- **Merciless**: This creature will prefer to execute downed opponents if possible.
- **Humiliation**: This creature will prefer to execute downed opponents if possible. The creature will not only kill the player but also will mutilate the corpse/destroy an item or something else that will stay with them if they are revived.
- **Devourer**: This creature can eat characters, killing them and earning bonuses for doing so.
- **Hopeless**: The ultimate form of Ruthless. This creature will attempt to create situations where it is impossible to escape death, will seek to ensure a characters death even at great detriment to itself, and when this occurs the roll to determine your fate cannot be modified in anyway. No Faith, No Inspiration, No Lucky. Only the base roll. Lastly, it may also have some way to make a character lose their will to fight.
- **Merciful**: The creature will attempt to not execute downed players. This may mean unconscious characters are somehow unable to return to the battle. In addition, the whole party being defeated will never result in a TPK.
- **Selective (Merciful/Ruthless)**: The creature has specific targets it will attempt to spare/kill once downed.
- **Playful (Merciful + Potential + Ignorance)**: This creature will attempt to not execute downed players and martial checks cannot reveal all of their abilities.

## Clashing
- **Martial Adept**: The creature is a martial weapon specialist, and can cause martial clashes.
- **Martial Master**: Martial clashes can occur. Martial Master means that this creature has advantage on all clashes of this nature. In addition, they may clash at will.
- **Magic Adept**: The creature is an arcane specialist, and can cause arcane clashes.
- **Magic Master**: Arcane clashes can occur. Arcane Master means that this creature has advantage on all clashes of this nature. In addition, they may clash at will.
- **Supernatural Adept**: This enemy has access to supernatural abilities, which means that it can be clashed with most martial and magical abilities or effects.
- **Bardic Adept**: This creature specializes in Bardic combat and can be engaged in bardic clashes.
- **Shared**: Attempting to clash against one enemy will cause all other enemies to participate, regardless of distance.
- **Grim Clash**: Whenever a player successfully clashes the enemy, they also lose. If they fail, they fail twice (in both cases, the extra fails are minor).
- **Dangerous Clash**: Any loss, even if minor, results in death.
- **Grand Clash**: This enemy is available for every single clash type. It has advantage on every single clash type. The cost of failure, no matter the degree, is always death.

## Combo
- **Combo**: This creature has abilities that synergize with each other.
- **Improved Combo**: The abilities can be purposefully comboed, the enemy can set up their ability to combo with other abilities, not tied to randomness.
- **Greater Combo**: Allows up to 3 layers of synergistic abilities. This trait allows for abilities to be set up in a way that they combo with one another more easily.
- **Grand Combo**: Like Combo except up to 7 layers.
- **Momentum (Requires any form of combo + any form of Martial Adept)**: This creature has access to Improved Combo, and has the ability to permanently change the fight’s pacing through his combo abilities. It can change phases/stances through comboing abilities. (Does not need to manually do it).
- **Synergy**: The creatures work as a team, benefiting each other.

## Intelligence
- **Cunning**: This creature will not deliberately make mistakes.
- **Greater Cunning**: The enemy will act taking into consideration the DM's understanding of not only the characters' but also the players' personality.
- **Tactician**: If you take an action, you cannot go back once your turn is passed (no going back if you forgot a bonus action and such. Only exemption is if a required effect needed to go off or illegal actions were taken). Creatures' strategy also accounts for player decisions, not just what would be expected from the player-characters.
- **Advanced Tactician**: Square by square movement. If you take an action, you cannot go back once your turn is passed (no going back if you forgot a bonus action and such. Only exemption is if a required effect needed to go off or illegal actions were taken). Creatures' strategy also accounts for player decisions, not just what would be expected from the player-characters.
- **Quickened Tactician**: There is an enforced time limit on your turns. Martial non-casters have one minute. Casters have two minutes.
- **Greater Tactician (Advanced Tactician + Quickened Tactician)**: Square by square movement. If you take an action, you cannot go back once your turn is passed (no going back if you forgot a bonus action and such. Only exemption is if a required effect needed to go off or illegal actions were taken). Creatures' strategy also accounts for player decisions, not just what would be expected from the player-characters. There is an enforced time limit on your turns. Martial non-casters have one minute. Half-Casters have 1 minute and 30 seconds. Casters have two minutes.
- **Foresight**: At the start of the turn, DM tries to predict the party's actions. If he predicts correctly, the enemy gets "advantages".
- **Remnant**: This creature may switch between acting tactically and being overcome by instincts.

## Knowledge
- **Delay**: This creature’s traits remain hidden on the first or more rounds.
- **Ignorance**: This creature has the ability to block or contest Martial or Knowledge based checks targeting it.
- **Greater Ignorance**: Martial does not reveal most of this creature's abilities. There may be another method to reveal them.
- **Unpredictable**: This creature’s intentions cannot be read through checks.
- **Potential**: This creature does not start the battle at its full potential.
- **Adaptive**: This creature can gain and lose traits.
- **Legend**: You face a living legend. This creature possesses a legendary ability. These are usually hidden and can only be revealed with Nat 20s in Martial checks.

## Lingering Effects
- **Lingering**: This creature has the ability to cause effects that persist even after its defeat.
- **Permanent**: This creature has the ability to cause effects that persist even after its death.
- **Life-changing**: The ultimate form of Lingering. This creature has the ability to cause effects that are un-removable.
- **Spell Burn**: This creature can sunder (break) magic spells, also may have some ability to seal away a caster's magic forever.
- **Destroyer**: The creature has the ability to destroy magic items.
- **Maiming**: This creature can sever limbs, permanently crippling victims. Such injuries may prevent full recovery after the battle, and in some cases, these injuries may render resurrection attempts ineffective unless the highest forms of restoration magic are used.

## Tactics
- **Lair**: This creature possesses lair actions.
- **Trapper**: This creature has the ability to passively inflict negative effects against creatures who perform specific actions.
- **Lord**: This creature does not fight alone and can command and sometimes summon other creatures. These creatures may or may not lose their will to fight or disappear after their leader is defeated or killed.
- **Swarm**: The creatures will prioritize attacking a single person over spreading their damage. They will surround that target even if it would put themselves at great risk.
- **Honorable**: This creature will give the players one round to prepare and get ready. However, attempting to leave will increase the creature’s power and give it a free round of attacks.
- **Honor Bound**: This enemy will not engage the players until they are fully prepared.
- **Benevolent**: This enemy will accept a surrender from the players, but surrendering might have consequences.

## Healing
- **Recovery**: This creature has the ability to recover a sizable amount of health. Can reduce the effectiveness of afflictions.
- **Recovery Adept**: This creature has the ability to restore large amounts of hit points on command.
- **Undying**: This creature has one or more ways to resist death.
- **Last Stand**: This creature does not die immediately when reduced to 0 hit points.
- **Twinned**: The creatures share a health pool or another kind of counter that they may equalize at any time.

## Debuffs
- **Afflicter**: This creature can cause status effects, often stackable.
- **Overcurse**: This creature can reduce stats below 0 (like Attack Bonus, Strength, etc.)
- **Sunderer**: This creature can remove benefits and deny class features.
- **Morale Breaker**: This creature possesses large amounts of crowd control effects, such as movement denial, and action skipping.

## Combat
- **Superior**: This creature has the ability to overwhelm its enemy magically, possessing abilities and attacks that have no saves and no chance of failure.
- **Overwhelming**: This creature has the ability to overwhelm its enemy physically, possessing abilities and attacks that have no saves and no chance of failure.
- **Pinnacle**: This creature has the ability to overwhelm its enemy using supernatural power, possessing abilities and attacks that have no saves and no chance of failure.
- **Deadly**: This creature has abilities that deal a higher tier of damage.
- **Improved Deadly**: Not much to say. Don't get hit. (Abilities that deal a higher tier of damage. May have several objectives or be very hard to avoid).
- **Lethal**: This creature has abilities that deal a higher tier of damage and can also cause failed death saving throws, and sometimes death.
- **Improved Lethal**: Any time wasted analyzing this creature is one less chance to run away, you know. (Abilities that deal extremely high damage and cause death saving throws or death)
- **Faithful**: This creature is a servant of a god that is actively participating in the fight.

## Buffs
- **Magic Resistance**: At certain times, this creature will be immune to all magical interference, damage or effects.
- **Defender**: This creature has a way to raise its defenses or defend itself. Attack negation and AC.

## Other
- **Sorrowful**: This creature has abilities that can potentially heal or strengthen the PCs.
- **Traveler’s Bond**: This represents a bond between long time travelling companions. These individuals have a bond and can cast spells on each other as if they were on themselves.
- **Merchant Fighter**: These individuals have access to many different resources and items that they can use from their merchandise/goods.

## Unique Traits (Owners)

- **Rewriter**: A Rewriter is not chosen by Hyperion, nor do they inherit the sacred right to carve Fables into reality. Through unyielding will, they force the world to acknowledge them. Where Hyperionids inscribe their Fables upon existence, a Rewriter rends the parchment itself—reshaping the tale not by legacy, but by conviction. They reject fate, deny prophecy, and scrawl new lines across the tapestry of existence, dragging history after them. This creature may twist events as they unfold, bend cause and consequence, and redirect outcomes to fit their vision. Expect the battle—and even the world around it—to shift in ways beyond prediction. Plans may dissolve, certainties may unravel, and what was once impossible may become inevitable. The limits of such power are unknown. No plan is safe from revision.
- **Betrayer**: An entity completely detached from the natural order of Yggdrasil and Lord Death's love, serving the Traitor, The One who betrayed Life itself. It exists outside the tapestry of existence, severed from the harmonious embrace of Faerun's design. This creature possesses the power to modify itself or be modified by The Traitor in the heat of battle, act as a conduit for knowledge to The Traitor, and even exerting influence not only over characters but also over the players themselves. Remember: Knowledge is power, and knowledge is the dominion of The Ancient. The Armageddon, known as The Hour of Devastation, beckons.
- **Shimmering**: This creature is deeply attuned to its memories, causing it to resonate with their Memoria Colectiva. While within The Heavens Tower, this resonance allows the creature to gain new abilities that enhance or synergize with its Memoria Colectiva. These abilities can include mana recovery under specific conditions or the modification of effects tied to the creature's Memoria Colectiva cards.
