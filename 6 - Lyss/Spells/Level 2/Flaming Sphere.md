| You create a 5-foot-diameter sphere of fire in an unoccupied space on the ground within range. It lasts for the duration. Any creature that ends its turn within 5 feet of the sphere makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.<br><br>As a [Bonus Action](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#bonus%20action_xphb), you can move the sphere up to 30 feet, rolling it along the ground. If you move the sphere into a creature's space, that creature makes the save against the sphere, and the sphere stops moving for the turn.<br><br>When you move the sphere, you can direct it over barriers up to 5 feet tall and jump it across pits up to 10 feet wide. Flammable objects that aren't being worn or carried start [burning](https://5e.tools/trapshazards.html#burning_xphb) if touched by the sphere, and it sheds [Bright Light](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#bright%20light_xphb) in a 20-foot radius and [Dim Light](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#dim%20light_xphb) for an additional 20 feet. |
