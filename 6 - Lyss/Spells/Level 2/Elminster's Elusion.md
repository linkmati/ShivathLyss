| **Range:** Self                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Components:** V, S                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

| Arcane wards protect you against magic for the duration. You have [Advantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#advantage_xphb) on saving throws against spells and magical effects. Additionally, if you succeed on a saving throw against a spell or magical effect and would normally take half as much damage, you instead take no damage. |
