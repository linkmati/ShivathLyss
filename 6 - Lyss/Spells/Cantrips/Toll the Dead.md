
| _Necromancy Cantrip_                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Casting Time:** Action                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Range:** 60 feet                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Components:** V, S                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Duration:** Instantaneous                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| You point at one creature you can see within range, and the single chime of a dolorous bell is audible within 10 feet of the target. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or take 1d8 Necrotic damage. If the target is missing any of its [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb), it instead takes 1d12 Necrotic damage. |
