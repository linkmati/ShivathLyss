| You gain the service of a familiar, a spirit that takes an animal form you choose: [Bat](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#bat_xmm), [Cat](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#cat_xmm), [Frog](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#frog_xmm), [Hawk](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#hawk_xmm), [Lizard](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#lizard_xmm), [Octopus](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#octopus_xmm), [Owl](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#owl_xmm), [Rat](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#rat_xmm), [Raven](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#raven_xmm), [Spider](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#spider_xmm), [Weasel](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#weasel_xmm), or another Beast that has a [Challenge Rating of 0](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#blankhash,flstchallenge%20rating:min=&0~max=&0,flsttype:beast=1,flstmiscellaneous:swarm=2). Appearing in an unoccupied space within range, the familiar has the statistics of the chosen form, though it is a Celestial, Fey, or Fiend (your choice) instead of a Beast. Your familiar acts independently of you, but it obeys your commands.<br><br>Telepathic Connection. While your familiar is within 100 feet of you, you can communicate with it telepathically. Additionally, as a [Bonus Action](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#bonus%20action_xphb), you can see through the familiar's eyes and hear what it hears until the start of your next turn, gaining the benefits of any special senses it has.<br><br>Finally, when you cast a spell with a range of touch, your familiar can deliver the touch. Your familiar must be within 100 feet of you, and it must take a [Reaction](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#reaction_xphb) to deliver the touch when you cast the spell.<br><br>Combat. The familiar is an ally to you and your allies. It rolls its own [Initiative](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#initiative_xphb) and acts on its own turn. A familiar can't attack, but it can take other actions as normal.<br><br>Disappearance of the Familiar. When the familiar drops to 0 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb), it disappears. It reappears after you cast this spell again. As a [Magic](https://5e.tools/actions.html#magic_xphb) action, you can temporarily dismiss the familiar to a pocket dimension. Alternatively, you can dismiss it forever. As a [Magic](https://5e.tools/actions.html#magic_xphb) action while it is temporarily dismissed, you can cause it to reappear in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you. Whenever the familiar drops to 0 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) or disappears into the pocket dimension, it leaves behind in its space anything it was wearing or carrying.<br><br>One Familiar Only. You can't have more than one familiar at a time. If you cast this spell while you have a familiar, you instead cause it to adopt a new eligible form. |
