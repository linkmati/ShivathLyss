Magic Item, Rare, requires attunement

The Ring of the Solipsis Archive is a training artifact granted to all initiates of the faction. It serves as an introduction to cognitive manipulation, teaching users how to weaponize their perception—either by altering their own understanding of reality or by influencing the minds of others.

This ring does not grant the ability to steal knowledge yet. Instead, it provides the fundamental techniques required to master the paths of Endurers (those who reshape their own limitations) and Orators (those who distort the perceptions of others).

## Mechanics Solipsis Initiate:
If your Intelligence score is lower than 13, it becomes 13 while you are attuned to this ring. 
The ring has a number of charges equal to your Intelligence modifier, with a minimum of 1 charge. It regains all expended charges when you finish a Long Rest.

## Forensic Cognition: 
You also always have the Encode Thoughts cantrip and the Detect Thoughts spell prepared. You can cast Detect Thoughts once without expending a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it this way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast it by expending a spell slot as normal. 

## Finding the Truth:
(Bonus Action, 1 Charge)
You get a bonus equal to your PB for every check of either INT, CHA or WIS (you may only choose one), this bonus last for a minute or until you use this ability again, whichever is earlier. 

## Confabulation
(1 Charge, Reaction, when you are about to take damage, before the damage is rolled.) 
Instead of rolling for damage, each die is treated as the minimum possible value, any additional effects of the attack or spell are negated. If the damage that you take would drop you below 0 hp, you remain at 1 but suffer any adverse effects as normal. 

## Paramnesia 
(1 Charge, Triggered Action, Once per turn) 
Trigger: When you target a creature with a melee or ranged attack. 
The attack hits automatically, regardless of the attack roll and deals additional damage equal to your intelligence modifier, unless the target is immune to the charmed condition or psychic damage. Charmed creatures don't lose the charmed condition from this damage.
