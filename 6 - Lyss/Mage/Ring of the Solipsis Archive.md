Magic Item, Rare, requires attunement

The Ring of the Solipsis Archive is a training artifact granted to all initiates of the faction. It serves as an introduction to cognitive manipulation, teaching users how to weaponize their perception—either by altering their own understanding of reality or by influencing the minds of others.

This ring does not grant the ability to steal knowledge yet. Instead, it provides the fundamental techniques required to master the paths of Endurers (those who reshape their own limitations) and Orators (those who distort the perceptions of others).

Intelligence Boost (Passive): If your INT is lower than 13, it is raised to 13 while attuned.

- **Forensic Cognition (Passive)**: 
	The wearer of this ring has the Detect Thoughts Spell always prepared. You can cast it once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest.
	
	The Ring of the Solipsis Archive grants its wearer to instinctively see the logical connections between pieces of information, to mentally reconstruct events, analyze causality, and connect fragmented information into a coherent whole, almost as if lines of thought were visibly linking one clue to another in their mind. This effect manifests in the form of heightened visualization, akin to a mental blueprint of logic, where the wearer sees the flow of cause and effect laid out before them.
	
	When making an Intelligence check that requires logical deduction, connecting evidence, reconstructing past events, or piecing together multiple sources of information, the DC is reduced by your Proficiency Bonus. **This also applies to Arcana (Intelligence) or Martial (Wisdom) checks for combat purposes**. 

- Shaping the Truth (Passive): 
	Not only does the wearer see reality for what it is, but they can also shape how others perceive it. When attempting to deceive, persuade, or manipulate others using the information discovered via Forensic Cognition, they gain an additional bonus equal to their Proficiency Bonus (PB) on Deception and Persuasion checks.

- Cognitive Reframing (Triggered Action, Once per Turn):
	Every user of the Archive learns to instinctively reshape the flow of battle by altering how an event is perceived. Whenever Cognitive Reframing is activated, the user makes a free Arcana or Martial check. This check does not have immediate effects but will determine the strength of related abilities once the initiate advances beyond the ring.

You may activate Cognitive Reframing when one of the following conditions occurs:
1. You are hit by an attack.
2. You land an attack on an enemy.

Then, choose one of the following adaptations, depending on your preferred method of mind control.

# Confabulation
Trigger: When you are hit by an attack or spell but before damage is rolled, or when an enemy misses an attack against you.

- Your mind rewrites the outcome of the attack, causing you to experience only the weakest possible version of the event rather than its full force. Instead of rolling for damage, each die is treated as the minimum possible value. For example, if the attack normally deals 8d6, instead of rolling, the damage is automatically 8 (1 per die). If the damage is a flat number, this ability reduces your damage by your INT modifier.
- If the attack would have missed, you can instead override the miss, voluntarily taking 1 point of damage but gaining additional temporary HP equal to your INT mod (stacks with other instances of temp HP).
- Critical Override: If the attack was a Critical Hit, you negate the effect, treating it as a normal hit.
- Survival Blind Spot: If this blow would reduce you to 0 HP, you remain at 1 HP instead (once per long rest). 


# Paramnesia
Trigger: When you hit or miss a creature with a melee or ranged attack.

Effect: You impose a false sensory imprint on the target, convincing them that they were struck even harder than they actually were—or at all.

- False Memory of Greater Pain: If your weapon attack included a damage die, and the roll was below its maximum value, you may retroactively "correct" the enemy’s perception by increasing the damage to its maximum possible roll.
- Mind-Altering Strike: If the enemy is currently affected by an Enchantment spell effect (such as Charmed, Frightened, Detect Thoughts, etc.), this attack deals double damage, as if it were a critical hit. The additional damage is psychic damage.  For the next round (until the end of your next turn), all your attacks gain +2 to attack rolls and +2 to damage rolls. Any attacks made during this effect, including the triggering attack, do not break enchantment conditions that would normally end when a target is attacked (e.g., Charmed, Frightened).
- If the attack would have missed, you may override the miss, treating it as if it landed as a glancing psychic strike that deals psychic damage and still triggers your Weapon Mastery effects.
