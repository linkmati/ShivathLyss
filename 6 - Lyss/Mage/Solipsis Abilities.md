# Solipsis Abilities
## Forensic Cognition: 
You also always have the Encode Thoughts cantrip and the Detect Thoughts spell prepared. You can cast Detect Thoughts once without expending a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it this way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast it by expending a spell slot as normal.

If you have no spellcasting ability from another source, Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for these spells and for the lamp’s abilities.
You can store a maximum number equal to your Spellcasting Modifier. All unspent Memories fade when you finish a Long Rest.

Once per turn, when you **fail a saving throw or take damage, you gain 1 Memory**. If an effect would trigger both, you gain 2 Memories.

## Finding the Truth 
#### (Bonus Action, 1 Memory)
Choose a skill check of either Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma. You may add your proficiency to any skill check done with that skill even if you already add proficiency to that skill check. This lasts for one minute or until you use this ability again. 

## Psychic Confession
You can feel the memories that linger within you, while Detect Thoughts is active you may target yourself, automatically failing the saving throw. When doing so, you may either reveal an uncomfortable memory or the DM may reveal a suppressed or embarrassing memory about your character. In doing so you gain 1 Memory, you may only use this ability once per long rest. (as you fail the save and do this, you gain 2 Memories)

## Confabulation
#### (1 Memory, Reaction, before the damage is rolled): 
Instead of rolling for damage, each die is treated as the minimum possible value, any additional effects of the attack or spell are negated. If the damage that you take would drop you below 0 hp, you remain at 1 but suffer any adverse effects as normal. 

## Endure 
#### (3 Memory, Reaction, Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma saving throw, before the effect takes place): 
If the spell does damage you take half damage if you fail or no damage if you succeed. If the spell has any additional effects you may take the damage to succeed the saving throw. If the n spell has no damage, you automatically succeed.

## NO NAME 
#### (2 Memory, Triggered Action, when a reaction you have access to may be triggered and you don't have a reaction available) 
You gain the reaction and make the reaction you are able to do

## Proyect Memories 
#### (1 Memory, Action that you make with a weapon or an unarmed weapon attack, 60ft)
You conjure part of your memories to deal 2d6+mod psychic damage. If the target is within 10ft of range, it instead takes 2d10+mod psychic damage. The attack uses your spellcasting ability for the attack and damage rolls instead of using Strength or Dexterity.
