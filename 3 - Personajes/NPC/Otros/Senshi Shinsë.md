# Senshi Shinsë

Senshi Shinsë is a liaison of the Registry who specializes in dungeon management, both in exploitation tasks (resource extraction, item appraisal) and exploration tasks (cartography, risk assessment). 

Shenshi does not only assign missions, he is often found in the back room butchering monsters, cooking them, or transforming their parts into equipment and consumables. His mastery allows him to extract the maximum value from every creature, turning scales into light armor, poisons into potions, and meat into high-calorie rations.

Born in a small treftelian village, he survived in his youth a dungeon collapse that wiped out his entire group. Forced to survive, he learned how to hunt, cook, and make use of every being he came across. He rose through the Registry thanks to his “Shinshe Method,” a zone rotation system that reduced conflicts between guilds. 

He always wears a reinforced leather apron over his robe and carries a ladle and a black “shield.” He is reserved and does not speak about his past. If something does not concern his work, he simply ignores it (unless it has something to do with monsters).

He appears when he is needed (with a ladle in one hand and a cup of monster broth in the other) and disappears as soon as he finishes his report. He is rarely seen outside official duties.

If you accept one of his missions, do not refuse the ration he offers you.
