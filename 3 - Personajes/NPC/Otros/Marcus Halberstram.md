# Marcus Halberstram

Uh, about me? Honestly, I'm not sure what to tell you. I've always wondered what it'd feel like to be on the other end of one of these articles, and now that it's actually my turn, I have no clue where to start.

Ok, name first, I guess. I'm Marcus Halberstram, or at least that's the name I go by inside the Registry. If I've learned anything in Shivath, it's that knowing a thing's — or a person's — true name is basically the same as owning them, so feel free to hold on to this one. Everyone in the Registry calls me Marcus anyway.

Doesn't really matter where I'm from or where I'm headed. I guess I can talk about what I actually do. So, for starters, my main ability is called — yeah, ok, not gonna give that away yet. My power's Order, let's leave it there, no real names. Basically my whole thing is setting rules, generally speaking. My job at the Registry, accordingly, is taking those rules, slapping them onto contracts, and binding you to the rules I've written across the world. It's a ridiculously strong ability, super useful, though it does have limits, full disclosure. Honestly, I think my wonderful husband's is way stronger than mine, but he hasn't let me say a word about his powers. Tough luck.

## The Grand Ledger

About my job at the Registry: aside from drafting your contracts (and making sure you forget you're bound to them), I'm also the one who built and runs my little intel-sharing platform, The Grand Ledger — yeah, the platform you're using to read this article. What do you think? Don't forget to [file incident reports](/feedback) if you spot any problems.

And now that you've read this and you're in on our little secret... in return, I've set up a spot where you can tell us what you think your contract is. I'm calling it the [Petition Well](/pozo), because basically you make petitions and they go straight into the void — the contract you signed is already signed, whether you like it or not. Kidding. Feedback's always welcome.

The whole point of The Grand Ledger is to be your field ops hub: make the work a bit less painful, keep intel-gathering on your missions from being a total nightmare. Pretty cool, huh?

I'll be adding articles bit by bit with what I know about the world, but I've got WAY too many of your petitions pending, I can't keep up, people. Maybe I should ask [[Fódhla]] for some time off...
