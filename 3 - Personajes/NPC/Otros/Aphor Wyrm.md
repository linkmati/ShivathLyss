# Aphor Wyrm

[[Aphor Wyrm]] or the Paper Claw is the leader or Novel of [[the Paper Menagerie]] a faction situated in [[Treftiel]] and [[Dho]]. 
She is a woman who has an entire arm made of Paper from the Pinus veritas, paper from a Pine tree that grows on the coastline which is specially susceptible to the local magical phenomenon.
She is highly proficient in using the magic native to Ilveris.
