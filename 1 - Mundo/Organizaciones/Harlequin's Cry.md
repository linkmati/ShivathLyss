#Organizations #Treftiel
A nihilistic faction that sees chaos and destruction as art, Harlequin’s Cry thrives on fear and anarchy. Their members dress as grotesque performers and fight with improvised weapons, turning every skirmish into a violent spectacle. Explosions, mind games, and illusions are their preferred tools of chaos.

Both feared and ridiculed, Harlequin’s Cry is a destabilizing force in Kaelor. Moriah, their leader, orchestrates their macabre performances to challenge the system and entertain their twisted followers.
