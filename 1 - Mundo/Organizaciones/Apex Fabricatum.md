#Organizations #Treftiel
The **Apex Fabricatum** is the most powerful and influential organization in **Treftiel**, combining both **religious and technological** authority. It is both a **church** and a **technocracy**, where the worship of technology, progress, and innovation takes center stage. Members of the Apex Fabricatum believe that technological advancement is not just progress, but a **sacred duty**, viewing machines and innovation as divine manifestations of an eternal pursuit toward perfection.

**Leadership and Structure**:

  
**- The Supreme Gearmaster**  is the highest authority, acting as both the political leader of Treftiel and the spiritual head of the Apex Fabricatum.

  
- The **Council of High Gears** is composed of top leaders who oversee specific sectors of technology and society. Each **High Gear** is responsible for an essential area like manufacturing, energy, communication, biotech, and transport.

- Beneath them are **Lesser Gears** and **Subgears**, who manage regional governance and specific technological projects.

**Doctrine of Progress**:

- The Apex Fabricatum teaches that technology is **sacred**, and innovation is the true path to advancement. They believe that every invention, machine, and piece of technology plays a role in the broader scheme of divine progress.
- **Religious ceremonies** in Treftiel often involve the unveiling of new technological advances, seen as blessings for the people.
- **Orders and Organizations**: Several **Orders** operate under the Apex Fabricatum, each specializing in areas like crafting technology (Order of Artificers), maintaining existing systems (Order of Mechanics), and biotechnological fusion (Order of Bioforgers). The **Luminarists** are a special group focused on studying and harnessing light and energy.

**Technological Supremacy**:

- The Apex Fabricatum is deeply intertwined with the governance of Treftiel, making them the most powerful faction in the continent.
- While they hold technological advancement above all, they are often in tension with groups that believe in the preservation of **primal magic** or **natural elements**. Some consider the Apex Fabricatum's push for technological dominance as **disruptive to the natural balance**.

**Symbolism**:

- **Gears** are central to their iconography, symbolizing how each individual, invention, and idea is a vital part of the larger machine of society. They wear futuristic vestments adorned with metallic gears and luminescent circuits, reflecting their alignment with both tradition and futuristic innovation.

**Impact on Society**:

- The influence of the Apex Fabricatum has turned Vitra and much of Treftiel into a **technological hub**, where citizens strive to align themselves with the ideals of progress. The **techno-elite** of society wield power and status based on their contributions to technological advancement.

**Magical Influence**:

- While their primary focus is on technology, the Apex Fabricatum acknowledges and sometimes incorporates **arcane** and **divine magic**, using it to augment technological creations or maintain control. However, the tension between **primal magic** (linked to nature) and technological progress often causes friction within certain groups in Treftiel.
