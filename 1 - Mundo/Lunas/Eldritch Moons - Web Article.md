# Eldritch Moons of Shivath: The Celestial Calendar

The night sky of Shivath is governed by the cyclical return of the **Eldritch Moons**. These rare and terrifying celestial phenomena warp the laws of nature, alter the minds of the living, and blur the boundaries between planes. 

Dates fluctuate annually within a flexibility margin of **+/- 1 day**.

---

## Spring

### First Month, Day 10: Glowering Moon
The night of despair. Silent voices whisper surrender under a suffocating blanket of hopelessness, sapping the willpower of those who dare look up.

### First Month, Day 20: Shattered Moon (First Occurrence)
Its broken light is strangely calm, but the shedding fragments still hunger to become whole again, drifting silently across the land.

### First Month, Day 40: Krakenlight (First Occurrence)
Water hangs in the sky like a suspended sea. Constant rain floods the streets, and deep monstrosities take flight as gravity fails.

### Second Month, Day 18: Creeping Tarlight
A deluge of dark, sticky tar plummets from the moon's face, coating the city in a viscous, oppressive dread.

### Second Month, Day 33: Slumbering Moon (First Occurrence)
Distance, memory, and intention twist. Shivath becomes a dream that refuses to let anyone leave or wake.

---

## Summer

### First Month, Day 8: Yellow Crown (First Occurrence)
A carved sign of elder power turns the city into a dim, haunted echo where light acts as both a revelation and a punishment.

### First Month, Day 25: Scorching Moon
A burning omen. The false inferno of illusory heat conceals real columns of erupting flames that punish the unwary.

### First Month, Day 40: Shattered Moon (Second Occurrence)
The silent ruins of the moon return to bring a brief period of peace and spell-dispelling celestial rain.

### Second Month, Day 15: Blood Moon of Rebirth
The Triduum of the Hunt. The lunar orb turns deep crimson and the recently deceased rise to join a feral, blood-fueled chase.

### Second Month, Day 33: Howling Moon (First Occurrence)
Echoing cries fill the night as feral, predatory instincts awaken in beasts and humanoids alike.

---

## Autumn

### First Month, Day 8: Slumbering Moon (Second Occurrence)
The haze of sleep blocks the borders again, erasing all thoughts and plans of leaving the city from memory.

### First Month, Day 22: Sanguine Eclipse
A red haze turns love to violence and trust to paranoia. Closest allies turn on each other in a frenzy.

### First Month, Day 39: Shattered Moon (Third Occurrence)
Silver shards drift through the fading autumn twilight, reflecting lost memories.

### Second Month, Day 14: Howling Moon (Second Occurrence)
Predatory cries ride the cold autumn winds, stirring wild beasts to migrate and hunt.

### Second Month, Day 33: Vacuous Moon
A wound in the sky that swallows light, thinning the boundary between the living and the dead to reveal chained apparitions.

---

## Winter

### First Month, Day 8: Krakenlight (Second Occurrence)
Suspended tides and heavy rains return as the deep frost sets in, flooding the icy streets.

### First Month, Day 24: Glacial Moon
An unyielding blizzard that saps warmth from bones and clarity from thoughts, leaving only frostbite.

### First Month, Day 41: Shattered Moon (Fourth Occurrence)
Quiet celestial light and soft silver dust fall before the end of winter.

### Second Month, Day 16: Yellow Crown (Second Occurrence)
Overcharged light illuminates frozen streets, casting visions of an older, grander city behind the veil.

### Second Month, Day 34: Slumbering Moon (Third Occurrence)
The final letargo of the calendar year seals the dreams of the cycle, preparing the world for spring.
