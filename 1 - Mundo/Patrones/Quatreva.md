# Quatreva

**Type:** Patron (Archfey) / Patron (Genie)  
**Subtitle:** The Lady of Four Faces  
**Pronouns:** She, He/They/It  

At the very beginning, time was crafted as a flawless rhythm, unchanging and eternal. But the seasons—wild and untamed—refused to conform. Spring grew without restraint, summer scorched the lands, autumn decayed endlessly, and winter froze the world in place. To restore order, **Quatreva was created**, the embodiment of the seasons' chaos.

They exiled her to [Valineth](/dossiers/valineth), the moon of eternal dusk, to contain the seasons’ imperfections. From there, her influence stretches to Shivath, shaping the cycles of nature. Yet this duty comes at a cost—**Quatreva’s essence shatters with each passing season**, leaving her fragmented: hopeful in spring, passionate in summer, melancholic in autumn, and despairing in winter.

Legends say if Quatreva’s fragments are ever united, the balance of the seasons will break, unleashing chaos or birthing a new harmony. Her warlocks, bound to one of her four faces, act in her name, though even they do not know her true purpose.

Now, she waits in Valineth, her throne heavy with sorrow, a prisoner of her duty and a reminder that even the perfect flow of time depends on the sacrifice of imperfection.

_"When her fragments unite, the moon will weep, Shivath will burn, and time itself will bend to the will of the seasons."_
